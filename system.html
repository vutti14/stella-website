The System

ระบบ — สิ่งที่อยู่ระหว่างคุณกับโลกภายนอก

ANATOMY

Eight layers between you and Bangkok.

  • I Outer pane 6mm tempered low-E
  • II Argon-filled cavity 12mm warm-edge spacer
  • III Inner pane 6mm tempered clear
  • IV Aluminum frame Thermally broken extrusion
  • V Triple gasket seal EPDM weather-rated
  • VI Hardware Roto / GU / Kinlong

Not all the layers are visible. The unseen ones do most of the work.

MATERIALS

The unseen majority.

Glass

Saint-Gobain / Guardian

Low-iron, low-emissivity, tempered. Each pane is checked for distortion before it leaves the line. Clear is not the same as honest.

Aluminum

Thermally broken extrusion

Profiles drawn to ±0.3mm tolerance. Anodized or powder-coated. Polyamide 66 thermal breaks block conduction across the frame.

Hardware

Roto NT / GU / Kinlong

Multi-point locking systems rated for 50,000 operating cycles. Hinges that hold a 200kg sash without sag for thirty years.
