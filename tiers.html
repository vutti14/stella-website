CHOOSING

How to choose your tier.

If you live

on a quiet soi, low floor, away from main roads

Essential is the right standard. Honest insulation, real silence, the system you'd specify in any new home that wasn't already done.

If you live

in a typical Bangkok premium home

Signature is what we recommend, and what we install in our own homes. The 1.4 W/m²K U-value is the inflection point where comfort, silence and economics align.

If you live

on a high floor, or facing west, or above a busy road

Reserve. The triple glazing is not vanity — it is engineering for an environment that punishes anything less.
